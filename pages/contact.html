Frequently Asked Questions

📅 How far in advance should I book?

For bridal and large events, we recommend booking 6-8 weeks in advance. For other services, 1-2 weeks is typically available. We accept last-minute bookings subject to availability.

💰 What's your cancellation policy?

Free cancellations up to 7 days before your appointment. Cancellations within 7 days may incur a 50% fee. No-shows will be charged in full.

🎨 Do you offer makeup trials?

Yes! Makeup trials are highly recommended, especially for bridal services. Trials cost $75 and can be applied to your final service if you book.

💄 What's your payment method?

We accept cash, card, Venmo, PayPal, and bank transfers. A 50% deposit is required to secure your booking, with the balance due on the day of service.

🚗 Do you travel for services?

Yes! We offer on-site services for an additional $50 travel fee. This includes hotel rooms, homes, venues, and event spaces within 15 miles of our location.

❓ Do you accommodate sensitive skin or allergies?

Absolutely! Please mention any skin sensitivities, allergies, or concerns when booking. We use hypoallergenic products and can customize our services accordingly.

📸 Can I book you for a photoshoot?

Yes! We specialize in makeup for photography. Please provide details about your concept, aesthetic, and any specific looks you're envisioning.

👗 What should I wear to my appointment?

Wear comfortable, loose-fitting clothing that won't interfere with makeup application. A button-up or open-back top is ideal to avoid disturbing your makeup.
